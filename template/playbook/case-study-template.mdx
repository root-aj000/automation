# CASE STUDY PLAYBOOK (The Story)
# File: case-studies/{slug}.mdx
# 🏗️ The "Hero's Journey" Copy & Visual Guide

> **How to Use**: This guides you to write a Case Study that sells.
> **The Goal**: Make the reader jealous of the client's results.

---

## **1. Hero Section (The Promise)**
> **The Job**: Show the result immediately.

### **🧠 Copy Strategy: The "Big Number" Hook**
*   **Concept**: Start with the Climax of the movie.
*   **Formula**: [Client Name] + [Verb] + [Big Metric].
*   **Example**: "LogisticsCorp Saved $120,000/Year."

### **🎨 Visual Director Notes**
*   **Visual**: A **Split Composition**.
    *   **Background**: A darkened, high-res photo of a modern office or the Client's HQs.
    *   **Foreground**: A floating "Outcome Card" showing a graph spiking upwards with the text "+400% Efficiency".
    *   **Logo**: The Client's Logo in white, prominent at the top.

---

## **2. The Challenges (The Villain)**
> **The Job**: Agitate the pain of the "Before" state.

### **🧠 Copy Strategy: PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solution)**
*   **Definition**:
    *   **Problem**: X was broken.
    *   **Agitate**: It caused Y stress and Z cost.
    *   **Solution**: (Tease our service).
*   **What to Write**: "Before working with us, [Client] was drowning in spreadsheets. They spent 20 hours a week just copy-pasting..."

### **🎨 Visual Director Notes**
*   **Visual**: **"The Chaos Collage"**.
*   **Description**: An artistic arrangement of "Stressful" items:
    *   Messy Excel screenshots.
    *   Red email notifications ("Urgent!", "Error!").
    *   A clock showing late hours (e.g., 8 PM).
*   **Filter**: Desaturated or slightly bluish tint to imply "Sad/Cold".

---

## **3. The Solution (The Guide)**
> **The Job**: Show *how* we fixed it (The Service).

### **🧠 Copy Strategy: The Process**
*   **Step 1**: "We Mapped." (Consultation).
*   **Step 2**: "We Built." (Development - No tool names).
*   **Step 3**: "We Deployed." (Hosting).

### **🎨 Visual Director Notes**
*   **Visual**: **"The Blueprint"**.
*   **Description**: A stylized flowchart or schematic diagram glowing in primary colors.
*   **Animation**: Animated lines connecting point A to point B, showing data flowing smoothly. This represents the "Order" we brought.

---

## **4. The Results (The Treasure)**
> **The Job**: Prove the ROI.

### **🧠 Copy Strategy: Hard & Soft Metrics**
*   **Hard Metrics**: Money ($), Time (Hours), Volume (Runs).
    *   *Copy*: "Reduced cost to just 2rs per run."
*   **Soft Metrics**: Sanity, Reliability.
    *   *Copy*: "No more weekend support calls."

### **🎨 Visual Director Notes**
*   **Visual**: **"The Dashboard"**.
*   **Description**: A high-fidelity mockup of the Client's dashboard (from our service).
*   **Highlight**: Zoom in on a "Green" status bar or a "Total Savings" widget.
