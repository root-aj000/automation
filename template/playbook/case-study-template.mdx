## 2. The Challenge (The "Before" Hell)
> **Buyer Question**: *"Does this apply to me?"* (Ref 03)
> **Business Answer**: "Overwhelmed by manual tasks." (Ref 01)

*   **Framework**: **PAS** (Problem)

## 3. The Solution (The Service)
> **Buyer Question**: *"What exactly do you do?"* (Ref 03)
> **Business Answer**: We planned, designed, and hosted it. (Ref 01)

*   **Framework**: **Process**
*   **What to Write**:
    *   "They handed us their requirements."
    *   "Our team built a custom SIM AI workflow."
    *   "We deployed it on our secure servers within 48 hours."

## 4. The Results (ROI)
> **Buyer Question**: *"Is it worth the cost?"* (Ref 03)
> **Business Answer**: Cheaper than hiring. Faster than DIY. (Ref 01)

*   **Framework**: **Quantified Outcome**
*   **What to Write**:
