# 🏗️ The "Insider" Copy & Visual Guide

> **How to Use**: Use this for industry pages (e.g., "Automation for Accountants").

## **1. Hero Section**
### **🧠 Copy Strategy: Mirroring**
*   **Concept**: Use the exact vocabulary of that industry.
*   **Action**: Google the "Top 5 pain points of [Industry]". Use those words.
    *   *Example*: "Reconciliation" for Accountants. "Triage" for Doctors.

### **🎨 Visual Director Notes**
*   **Visual**: **The "Desk" Shot**.
*   **Description**: A top-down view (flat lay) of a desk typical for that industry.
    *   *Accountant*: Calculator, receipts, coffee.
    *   *Construction*: Blueprints, hard hat, tablet.
*   **Overlay**: A glowing "Automation Layer" hovering over the manual tools.
