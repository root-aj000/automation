# 🏗️ The "Educational Authority" Copy & Visual Guide

> **How to Use**: Use this for writing articles.
> **The Goal**: Teach, don't just sell.

---

## **1. The Headline**
### **🧠 Copy Strategy: Curiosity Gap**
*   **Formula**: [Number] ways to [Benefit] without [Pain].
*   **Example**: "3 Ways to Automate Billing without Hiring an Accountant."

### **🎨 Visual Director Notes**
*   **Visual**: **Editorial Illustration**.
*   **Style**: Flat, vector art style (like Notion or Corporate Memphis but verified premium).
*   **Subject**: Metaphorical representation of the topic (e.g., A person riding a rocket for "Speed").

---

## **2. The Intro**
### **🧠 Copy Strategy: APP (Agree-Promise-Preview)**
*   **Agree**: "Invoice chasing is the worst part of business."
*   **Promise**: "It doesn't have to be."
*   **Preview**: "Here is the exact system to fix it."
