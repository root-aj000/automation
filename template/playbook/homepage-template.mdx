# 🏗️ Homepage Copy Playbook [FULLY MANAGED MODEL]

> **Business Context**: You are NOT selling a tool (like Zapier). You are selling a **Service** that uses tools. The user does ZERO work.
> **Core Value**: "You tell us the process. We automate it end-to-end."

---

## 1. Hero Section 1 (Above the Fold)
> **Buyer Question**: *"Okay… what is this thing?"* (Ref 03)
> **Business Answer**: A fully managed service. We build, host, and maintain automations for you. (Ref 01)

*   **Framework**: **AIDA** (Attention → Interest)
*   **What to Write**:
    *   **Headline**: Solve the "Overwhelmed" problem.
        *   *Good*: "We Build & Manage Your AI Automation. You Just Focus on Growth."
        *   *Bad*: "The Best Automation Platform." (Wrong - we are a service).
    *   **Subheadline**: Explain the "Done-For-You" aspect. "No learning curve. No hiring engineers. Our team handles everything from design to hosting."
    *   **CTA**: "Get Your Free Automation Plan".

---

## 2. Social Proof / Logo Cloud
> **Buyer Question**: *"Who are you — a real company?"* (Ref 03)
> **Business Answer**: Creating a "Done-for-you" partnership. (Ref 01)

*   **Framework**: **Authority**
*   **What to Write**: "Trusted by 500+ businesses to handle their mission-critical workflows."

---

## 3. The "Status Quo" Problem (Hero 2)
> **Buyer Question**: *"Why should I care about this problem?"* (Ref 03)
> **Business Answer**: Manual tasks are costly. Hiring is expensive. DIY tools are confusing. (Ref 01)

*   **Framework**: **PAS** (Problem → Agitate → Solution)
*   **What to Write**:
    *   **Problem**: "Tried Zapier but got stuck? Tired of managing virtual assistants?"
    *   **Agitate**: "Every broken workflow costs you money and sleep."
    *   **Solution**: "Stop building. Start automating. We handle the tech so you don't have to."

---

## 4. How It Works (The Service Model)
> **Buyer Question**: *"Do I have to think too hard?"* (Ref 03)
> **Business Answer**: 1. Requirement -> 2. We Build -> 3. We Host. (Ref 01)

*   **Framework**: **Simplification (1-2-3)**
*   **What to Write (Strictly follow this flow)**:
    1.  **Tell Us**: "You explain your process."
    2.  **We Build**: "Our experts design and code the workflow "
    3.  **We Manage**: "We host it on our secure servers. You just see the results."

---

## 5. Value Proposition / Differentiators
> **Buyer Question**: *"Why should I choose you over Zapier/Freelancers?"* (Ref 03)
> **Business Answer**: Fully Managed, Unlimited Runs, Hosted Infrastructure. (Ref 01)

*   **Framework**: **Benefit Stacking**
*   **What to Write**:
    *   ❌ **No DIY**: "Never touch a workflow builder again."
    *   ⚡ **Hosted Infrastructure**: "Runs on our enterprise-grade servers, not your laptop."
    *   ♾️ **Unlimited Runs**: "Flat pricing. No surprise overage fees."

---

## 6. Case Studies
> **Buyer Question**: *"Show me proof."* (Ref 03)

*   **Framework**: **Result-First**
*   **What to Write**: "How [Client] saved 40 hours/week without hiring a single employee."

---

## 7. FAQ (Objection Handling)
> **Buyer Question**: *"Is this hard to set up? What if it breaks?"* (Ref 03)
> **Business Answer**: We handle maintenance. We fix bugs. (Ref 01)

*   **Required Q&A**:
    1.  **Q**: "Do I need to know how to code?"
        *   **A**: "Zero. We handle all the technical details."
    2.  **Q**: "What if an automation breaks?"
        *   **A**: "Our monitoring system alerts us instantly. We fix it before you even notice."
    3.  **Q**: "Is my data safe?"
        *   **A**: "Yes, we use bank-grade encryption on our private servers."

faq:
